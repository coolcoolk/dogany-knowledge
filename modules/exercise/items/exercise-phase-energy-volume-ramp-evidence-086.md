refraction_notes:
  - note: >
      A BIGGER SURPLUS BUYS FAT, NOT MUSCLE. In trained lifters a 15% surplus
      did not grow muscle or the squat more than 5% or maintenance over 8
      weeks, and fat rose with body-mass gain; in elite athletes a planned
      surplus doubled the weight gain and multiplied the fat gain while
      lean-mass gain did not differ. The direction is consistent; the trials
      are small and short. A "grow" phase is therefore a small surplus at
      most, and weight rising fast is a signal to slow it, not a sign of
      faster growth.
    grade: C
  - note: >
      THE GAIN RATE TARGET IS A COACHING NUMBER. About 0.25-0.5% of body
      weight a week for novice and intermediate lifters (less for advanced)
      comes from a narrative review, not from a trial of rates. The weekly
      reading of it must also clear the weight noise band (nutrition 029)
      before it is spoken as on or off target.
    grade: D
  - note: >
      THE USER'S OWN RECENT VOLUME IS THE REFERENCE. Trained men who simply
      kept their previous weekly sets grew as much as those who added 30% or
      60%, and kept the best strength; another trial found a 20% step above
      the person's own logged volume beat a fixed 22 sets. A third found 32
      sets beat 16 in trained men. Read together: start a new block from
      what the user actually did, add little, and never jump straight to a
      generic high number. None of these trials ran inside a deficit or a
      surplus.
    grade: C
  - note: >
      NO TRIAL SETS A RAMP RATE. How fast to raise volume after a cut, a
      maintenance block or a layoff is untested in lifting. The one
      fixed-percentage ramp trial (the running 10% rule) found no injury
      benefit. Any per-week step is a product constant, chosen to keep the
      first week back from being the sore one (repeated-bout effect), not
      because a faster ramp is known to harm.
    grade: D
  - note: >
      SIZE GOES IN MONTHS, STRENGTH STAYS. Twenty weeks off took muscle
      thickness back to baseline while strength stayed well above it; three
      weeks off between blocks cost nothing measurable overall. Short breaks
      need no special restart; a long one restarts volume low but not the
      load the user can still lift with good form.
    grade: C
claim: >
  The size of an energy surplus mostly decides how much fat comes with
  training, not how much muscle: in trained lifters a 15% surplus grew no
  more muscle than 5% or maintenance over eight weeks, and in elite
  athletes a planned surplus that doubled weight gain added fat without
  more lean mass. The commonly given 0.25-0.5% of body weight per week for
  a gaining phase is a coaching number. For volume, trained lifters who
  kept their own previous weekly sets grew as well as those who added 30%
  or 60%, and a 20% step above a person's own logged volume beat a fixed
  generic dose, although a separate trial found 32 weekly sets beat 16. No
  trial sets how fast to raise resistance-training volume after a phase
  switch or a layoff; size is lost over months off while strength largely
  stays, and the one tested fixed weekly ramp (running's 10% rule) did not
  prevent injury. The deficit side stays with 071 and 016.
reasoning: >
  Helms 2023 is the only randomised trial located that sets surplus size in
  trained lifters; it is small and lost subjects to COVID, so its
  regression (larger N) carries the conclusion, as the authors say. Garthe
  2013 points the same way in a different population with a
  counselling-driven surplus. Iraki 2019 and Slater 2019 are reviews that
  give the coaching rate and say the needed surplus is unknown. For
  volume, Barsuhn 2025 and Scarpelli 2022 both anchor the dose to the
  person's own recent volume, one by keeping it and one by a 20% step;
  Brigatto 2022 shows a higher absolute dose can add in trained men, so
  "more never helps" is not the reading -- "jumping far above what you
  did does not reliably help" is. Psilander 2019 and Ogasawara 2013 give
  the detraining time course. Hyldahl 2017 explains why unaccustomed
  volume is sorest the first time and protected after. Buist 2008 is the
  only trial of a fixed weekly ramp rule and it is null and in runners, so
  every ramp step stays a labelled product constant.

# exercise/phase-energy-volume-ramp-evidence-086 -- 증량·감량·유지 전환 때 볼륨과 칼로리 근거

**한 줄 그림:** 칼로리를 많이 남겨 먹는다고 근육이 더 붙지는 않고 지방이 더 붙는다. 볼륨은
최근에 실제로 하던 세트 수에서 시작해 조금씩만 올린다. 몇 주에 걸쳐 얼마씩 올릴지는 연구된 적이 없다.

## What the evidence says, by question

| Question | Finding | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| How big a surplus for a grow phase? | 5% did as well as 15% for muscle and squat; bigger surplus, more fat | Helms 2023 (n=17), Garthe 2013 (n=39) |
| What weight-gain rate to aim for? | about 0.25-0.5% body weight / week, less if advanced | review (coaching number) |
| Volume in the new block? | keeping previous weekly sets grew as well as +30 / +60%; +20% over own log beat a fixed 22 | Barsuhn 2025, Scarpelli 2022 |
| Can more absolute volume help? | 32 sets beat 16 in one trained trial | Brigatto 2022 |
| How fast to ramp after a switch or layoff? | untested in lifting; the running 10% rule did not prevent injury | Buist 2008 |
| What does a layoff cost? | size back to baseline after 20 weeks off, strength mostly kept; 3 weeks off cost nothing overall | Psilander 2019, Ogasawara 2013 |
| Deficit phase | see 071 (keep, not grow) and 016 (more sets do not protect) | -- |

## 한국어 요약 (답변용)

- 증량기에 칼로리를 크게 남긴다고 근육이 더 붙지는 않았다. 운동 경력자 실험에서 유지 칼로리,
  5% 초과, 15% 초과를 비교했을 때 근육 두께와 스쿼트는 차이가 없었고, 많이 먹은 쪽은 피하지방만
  더 늘었다. 그래서 증량은 "조금 남겨 먹기"가 기본이다.
- 증량 속도로 흔히 말하는 주당 체중의 0.25~0.5%는 코칭에서 쓰는 숫자이지, 실험으로 정해진 값은
  아니다. 한 주 체중 변화는 측정 오차보다 작을 때가 많아서 몇 주 평균으로 봐야 한다.
- 새 단계를 시작할 때 볼륨의 기준은 일반적인 권장 숫자가 아니라 사용자가 최근에 실제로 한 주간
  세트 수다. 그대로 유지해도 근육은 자랐고, 30%나 60%를 한꺼번에 올려도 더 자라지 않았다. 자기
  기록의 1.2배로 올린 쪽이 일률적인 22세트보다 나았다는 연구도 있다.
- 다만 세트를 많이 하면 더 자란다는 연구(주 32세트가 16세트보다 나음)도 있다. "더 하면 소용없다"가
  아니라 "한 번에 크게 올린다고 확실히 더 좋지는 않다"로 말한다.
- 감량이나 휴식 뒤에 볼륨을 얼마나 빨리 올려야 하는지는 근력운동에서 연구된 적이 없다. 달리기의
  "주 10%씩만 늘리기" 규칙도 부상을 줄이지 못했다. 단계적으로 올리는 이유는 첫 주 근육통을 줄이려는
  것이지, 빨리 올리면 다친다는 근거가 있어서가 아니다.
- 20주를 쉬면 근육 크기는 처음으로 돌아갔지만 힘은 상당 부분 남았다. 3주 정도 쉬는 건 전체 결과에
  별 손해가 없었다.
- 감량기 볼륨은 071(유지 목표, 무게는 지키기)과 016(세트를 늘려도 근육이 더 지켜지지 않음)을 따른다.
