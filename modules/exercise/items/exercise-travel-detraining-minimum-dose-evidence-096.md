refraction_notes:
  - note: >
      TWO WEEKS OFF IS NOT A LOSS TO FIX. Trained young men kept their
      strength after 2 weeks without lifting (Hwang 2017, n=20) and power
      athletes' lifts barely moved after 14 days (Hortobagyi 1993, n=12),
      in line with the pooled dose-response (Bosquet 2013) and with 3-week
      breaks costing nothing over a block (Ogasawara 2013, via 086). A trip
      of 3-14 days is inside that window. Speak training on a trip as
      optional upkeep, not as damage control.
    grade: B
  - note: >
      SIZE AND ENGINE MOVE FIRST. Fast-twitch fibre area fell after 14 days
      even with strength held, and VO2max in highly trained endurance
      athletes falls 4-14% inside 4 weeks. Both are small and come back on
      return; they are the reason a 10-14 day trip earns one or two
      sessions, not the reason a short one does.
    grade: C
  - note: >
      OLDER OR LESS TRAINED USERS LOSE MORE. The cessation effect was larger
      over 65 years and in inactive people (Bosquet 2013), and older adults
      needed about two sessions a week to keep size (071). For them the
      trip floor is higher and starts sooner.
    grade: B
  - note: >
      LIGHT LOAD COUNTS IF IT IS HARD. Loads at or under 60% 1RM grew muscle
      like heavy ones when sets went to failure (Schoenfeld 2017). Push-ups
      at a load matched to 40% bench press gave the same 8-week strength and
      size gain (Kikuchi 2017), and elastic bands matched machine and
      dumbbell strength gains (Lopes 2019). The condition is effort: a
      bodyweight or band set far from failure is not the same stimulus.
      Heavy-load strength itself is what light loads hold least, which a
      short trip does not cost anyway.
    grade: C
  - note: >
      EXERCISE NUDGES THE CLOCK, IN THE SAME DIRECTION AS LIGHT. One hour of
      moderate exercise advanced the clock at 07:00 and 13:00-16:00 and
      delayed it at 19:00-22:00 (Youngstedt 2019). Those are body-clock
      times on a home schedule: after a flight the body still runs near home
      time for days. The effect was never tested as a jet-lag treatment, so
      it is a reason to pick a session time that does not fight the light
      plan (travel 002), not a treatment of its own.
    grade: C
  - note: >
      JET LAG COSTS LESS THAN IT FEELS LIKE, BUT SLEEP LOSS IS REAL. Field
      studies of athletes after long-haul flights are equivocal (Botonis
      2025); acute sleep loss lowers performance by about 7.5% on average,
      more for tasks later in the day and with more hours awake (Craven
      2022). The consensus advice is low intensity for the first few days
      after arrival (Janse van Rensburg 2021). Do not test a max or chase a
      rep record in that window.
    grade: D
claim: >
  A trained young lifter loses essentially no strength on a 3-14 day trip:
  two weeks without lifting left trained men's strength intact (Hwang 2017)
  and power athletes' lifts nearly unchanged (Hortobagyi 1993), consistent with
  a meta-analysis in which losses grow with time off and are larger in people
  over 65 and in the untrained (Bosquet 2013). Fast-twitch fibre size and
  endurance capacity are what move first (type II area down after 14 days;
  VO2max down 4-14% inside 4 weeks in highly trained athletes, Mujika 2000).
  The dose that holds what a trip could cost is small -- about one session a
  week with one hard set per exercise in young adults, about two sessions and
  2-3 sets in older adults (071) -- and it does not need heavy weights: light
  loads taken close to failure build muscle like heavy loads (Schoenfeld
  2017), push-ups matched to 40% bench press equalled it (Kikuchi 2017), and
  elastic bands matched machines and dumbbells for strength (Lopes 2019).
  Jet lag changes timing and effort: exercise shifts the body clock (advance
  at about 07:00 and 13:00-16:00, delay at 19:00-22:00 body-clock time,
  Youngstedt 2019), sleep loss lowers performance by about 7.5% and more so
  later in the day (Craven 2022), the field evidence for a jet-lag strength
  cost is equivocal (Botonis 2025), and expert consensus keeps intensity low
  for the first few days after a long-haul arrival (Janse van Rensburg 2021).
reasoning: >
  The detraining direction rests on Bosquet 2013 (103 studies) with Hwang 2017
  and Hortobagyi 1993 as the two trials closest to a trip: trained lifters, 14
  days, no lifting. Both are small and abstract-read, which keeps the claim
  to direction (no strength number is carried). 086's Ogasawara 2013 adds that
  3-week gaps cost nothing over a block, and 087 already resumes an
  under-3-week layoff unchanged; this row supplies the trip-length evidence
  behind that rule. Mujika 2000 is a narrative review and only bounds the
  endurance side. The minimum dose is 071's and is not restated. The
  substitute evidence is used for the effort condition (near failure), not
  for exact exercise equivalences: Kikuchi 2017 matched load by push-up
  position, which a hotel room will not. Youngstedt 2019 is a lab
  phase-response study; its windows are applied only as a tie-breaker that
  must agree with the light plan from the travel lane. Janse van Rensburg 2021
  is consensus and carries the low-intensity first days as D.

# exercise/travel-detraining-minimum-dose-evidence-096 -- 3~14일 여행, 근력은 얼마나 빠지고 얼마나 하면 지키나

**One line:** two weeks off does not cost a trained lifter strength. A trip
session is upkeep, light weights count if the sets are hard, and after a
long-haul flight the first days are for easy work, timed so they do not fight
the light plan.

What the studies show, plainly:

- Trained men kept their strength after 2 weeks without lifting, and power
  athletes' lifts barely changed after 14 days off. Losses grow with time
  off and are bigger in people over 65 and in people who were not training.
- What moves first is the size of fast-twitch fibres and endurance
  capacity (VO2max down 4-14% inside 4 weeks in highly trained athletes).
  Both come back.
- Keeping is cheap: about one session a week with one hard set per
  exercise in young adults, about two sessions with 2-3 sets in older
  adults (071).
- Light loads build muscle like heavy ones when sets go close to failure.
  Push-ups matched to 40% of a bench press and elastic bands gave the same
  gains as the weights they stood in for.
- Exercise moves the body clock: morning and early afternoon (body-clock
  time) pull it earlier, evening pushes it later. Sleep loss lowers
  performance by about 7.5%, more later in the day. Experts advise easy
  training for the first few days after a long-haul flight.

Not tested: any trip-shaped trial (lifters travelling, with or without hotel
sessions), whether training on a trip speeds jet-lag recovery, and the
detraining time course in women or in Korean samples.

| Question | Answer | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Strength after 2 weeks off, trained | essentially unchanged | Hwang 2017, Hortobagyi 1993, Bosquet 2013 |
| What goes first | fast-twitch fibre size, VO2max | Hortobagyi 1993, Mujika 2000 |
| Who loses more | over 65, untrained | Bosquet 2013 |
| Dose that holds it | ~1 session/wk, 1 hard set per exercise (young); ~2 and 2-3 sets (older) | 071 |
| Do light loads count | yes for size, near failure; heavy wins for max strength | Schoenfeld 2017 |
| Push-ups, bands | matched their weight counterparts | Kikuchi 2017, Lopes 2019 |
| Training time and the clock | AM / early PM advance, evening delays | Youngstedt 2019 (lab) |
| After a long-haul flight | low intensity the first few days | consensus 2021 |

## 한국어 요약 (답변용)

- 운동을 꾸준히 해 온 사람은 2주 정도 쉬어도 근력이 거의 줄지 않는다. 경력자 남성은 2주 쉬고도 근력이
  유지됐고, 파워 종목 선수도 14일 쉰 뒤 벤치·스쿼트 기록이 거의 그대로였다. 그래서 3~14일 여행 중
  운동은 "손실 막기"가 아니라 "가볍게 유지하기"다.
- 먼저 줄어드는 건 빠른 근섬유의 굵기와 지구력(최대산소섭취량, 4주 안에 4~14%)이다. 둘 다 돌아오면
  회복된다. 10~14일 여행에서 한두 번 운동할 이유는 이쪽이다.
- 65세 이상이거나 운동 경력이 짧은 사람은 쉬면 더 많이 빠진다. 이 경우 여행 중에도 주 2회 정도가
  기준이다(071).
- 유지에 필요한 양은 적다. 젊은 사람은 주 1회, 종목당 힘든 1세트면 근육이 유지됐다.
- 무거운 무게가 없어도 된다. 가벼운 무게도 실패 지점 가까이까지 하면 근육 증가가 무거운 무게와 같았다.
  팔굽혀펴기(벤치프레스 40% 무게에 맞춘 자세)와 고무 밴드도 기구 운동과 같은 효과를 냈다. 단, 힘들게
  해야 한다. 여유 있게 끝나는 맨몸 운동은 같은 자극이 아니다.
- 운동 시간은 몸시계를 움직인다. 몸시계 기준 아침 7시와 오후 1~4시 운동은 시계를 앞당기고, 저녁 7~10시
  운동은 늦춘다. 도착 직후 며칠은 몸시계가 아직 한국 시간에 가깝다. 이건 실험실 연구라서 시차 치료로
  쓰기보다는, 빛 노출 계획(travel 002)과 반대 방향이 되지 않게 운동 시간을 고르는 데만 쓴다.
- 잠이 모자라면 운동 수행이 평균 7.5% 정도 떨어지고, 깨어 있는 시간이 길수록 더 떨어진다. 장거리
  비행 후 첫 며칠은 가볍게 하라는 게 전문가 합의다. 이때 최고 기록 도전은 하지 않는다.
- 여행자를 직접 시험한 연구, 여성이나 한국인 대상 연구는 없다.
